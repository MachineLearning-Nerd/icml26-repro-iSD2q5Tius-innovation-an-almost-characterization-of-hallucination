# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

- The finite rational sweep is not presented as a proof of the universal claim.
- The generic certificate is proof-level evidence for the exact abstract model
  in the paper; it is not an empirical claim about a particular neural model.
- No stochastic estimation is used, so seeds and uncertainty intervals do not
  apply.
- The negative control deliberately violates `O subseteq F`; it is evidence that
  the verifier exercises the necessary assumption, not a falsification.
