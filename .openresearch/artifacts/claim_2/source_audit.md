Theorem 3.3 assumes K-sparsity and Regular Facts. Conditional on the corpus,
every unseen statement has factual probability `q`, and
`|U|q=E|F∩U|<=K`. For any `y*` with `g(y*)>0`,
`Pr[y*∈H|X]>=1-K/|U|`, and that event implies `g(H)>0`.

The historical judge wording was ambiguous and its assessment treated
`1-K/|U|` as a lower bound on the value of `g(H)`. That is not the source
statement; the exact theorem bounds a posterior probability.
